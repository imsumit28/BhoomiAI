Legacy paper & scanned records – historical land records exist as handwritten registers,
scanned images, PDFs and maps that are not searchable or machine-readable
Poor document quality – faded ink, damaged pages, noise, skewed scans and inconsistent
layouts sharply reduce OCR reliability
Multilingual & handwritten text – records mix several Indian languages and scripts with
handwritten entries and annotations
Manual data entry – human transcription is slow, expensive and error-prone, creating
backlogs in land record offices
Unstructured land data – owner names, survey/khasra/khata numbers, areas, mutations and
classifications appear in varying formats
Validation & duplicates – digitized data must be checked for inconsistencies, duplicate
survey numbers and ownership conflicts
Disconnected systems – digital records need interoperability with LRMS, DILRMP,
GIS/cadastral maps and government databases
End-to-end AI pipeline – ingests scanned registers, handwritten pages, legacy PDFs and
maps, cleans them and converts multilingual text into structured land-record fields
Confidence-aware, never blind OCR – every field carries a confidence score and passes
rule, duplicate and cross-record / GIS checks before acceptance
Risk-based human verification – officers see only low-confidence or conflicting fields
beside the source image, then approve, correct or reject
Trusted, connected output – versioned records with audit trail are exposed via APIs to
LRMS, DILRMP and GIS; corrections continuously improve the models

TECHNICAL APPROACH
SIH Idea Submission  |  VIT Bhopal University
Methodology and Process
Technology Stack
BhoomiSetu AI System Workflow
React TypeScript Vite Tailwind CSS
Express MongoDB + Mongoose Socket.IO JWT
1. Document Ingestion – upload scanned PDFs, images, registers and
legacy files; originals kept in a secure archive
2. Image Enhancement – denoise, deskew, contrast-correct and crop
pages so faded or damaged scans become readable
3. Layout & Region Detection – detect tables, headers, form fields and
handwritten regions to preserve structure
4. Multilingual OCR – script detection routes printed and handwritten text
to suitable Indic OCR / HTR models
5. AI Field Extraction – NLP and layout-aware Document AI map text to
owner, survey / khasra / khata no., area, mutation
6. Confidence Scoring – each field gets a score; uncertain values are
highlighted, not trusted blindly
7. Validation Engine – business rules, duplicate detection and cross-record
database checks flag conflicts
8. Human Verification & Learning – officers fix low-confidence fields
side by side; corrections retrain the models
